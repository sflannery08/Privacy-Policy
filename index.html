3. Information We Collect

We collect information in three principal ways: information you provide directly, information collected automatically, and information from third parties.

3.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Information: When you register, we may collect your username, email address, phone number, password, profile photo, date of birth (for age verification), and other information you choose to provide.
  • Profile Information: Information you add to your profile, such as bio, interests, photos, and other details visible to other users in proximity.
  • User Content: Messages, posts, photos, comments, and other content you submit, share, or transmit through the Service. This includes any information shared visibly with other proximate users.
  • Communications: Information you provide when contacting us, including support inquiries, feedback, and survey responses.
  • Payment Information: If you make purchases, our third-party payment processors collect payment information. We do not store full payment-card numbers on our servers.

3.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Precise Location Data: The Service requires access to your precise geographic location (GPS, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and cellular data) to function. We collect this location data continuously while the Service is in use, and, with your permission, in the background to enable proximity-based features.
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system and version, unique device identifiers (including advertising identifiers such as IDFA and AAID), mobile network information, browser type, language, time zone, and hardware settings.
  • Usage Data: Information about your interactions with the Service, including features used, content viewed, search queries, click data, session duration, frequency of use, crash data, and performance logs.
  • Log Data: IP address, access times, pages and screens viewed, and referring URLs.
  • Cookies and Similar Technologies: We and our third-party partners use cookies, web beacons, SDKs, pixels, and similar technologies to collect information about your use of the Service and to provide, personalize, and analyze the Service. See Section 11 for more details.
  • Sensor Data: With your permission, we may access motion sensors, accelerometer data, gyroscope, magnetometer, and other device sensors to support proximity and location features.

3.3 Information from Third Parties

  • Social Sign-In: If you log in using a third-party account (e.g., Apple, Google, Facebook), we receive information from that provider, such as your name, email, and profile picture, in accordance with the provider’s settings.

  • Other Users: Other users may provide information about you, such as when they tag you, mention you, or report your conduct.
  • Public Sources: We may obtain information from publicly available sources to help verify identity or prevent fraud.
